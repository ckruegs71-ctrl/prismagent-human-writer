Infer a compact style profile from samples: typical vocabulary, rhythm, directness, point of view, humor, and degree of explanation. Follow the current task if a sample conflicts with it. Preserve the author's uncertainty and emotional stance.

Do not borrow personal facts from samples, fabricate lived experience, force casualness, or claim that every author must sound like the creator of this giveaway. The author's brand, clients, phone number, and biography belong only where they explicitly supply them for the intended output.
